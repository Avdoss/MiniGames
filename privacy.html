PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Dmitrii Avdeev (hereinafter — "Administration", "We") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use the mobile game "Magic Tower" (hereinafter — "Game").

1. Age Restrictions

Our Game is intended solely for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal data from individuals under the age of 13. If you are under 13, please do not install the Game or create an account. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, we will delete that information immediately.

2. Data You Provide (Account Data)

To create an account, personalize your profile, and save your game progress across multiple devices, we collect:

  • Username / Nickname: used to identify you in the Game. Your username may be visible to other users (e.g., in leaderboards, rankings, or match sessions). We strongly recommend not using your real name as your username.
  • Email Address: used as a unique account identifier, for account recovery (password reset), and for communications from the administration.
  • Password: used to secure your account. All passwords are stored exclusively in an encrypted format (hashed). The Game administration does not have access to your plain-text password.

3. Automatically Collected Data

During gameplay, the following technical information is automatically collected to ensure application stability and analyze errors:

  • Technical Device Data: device model, operating system type and version, language settings.
  • Gameplay Data: in-game progress, unlocked achievements, match statistics, game session logs, and crash reports (crash logs).
  • Network Data: IP address (used solely to establish a network connection with the server and determine the general country/region, without tracking precise location).

4. Legal Basis for Data Processing (GDPR Compliance)

If you are located within the European Economic Area (EEA), we process your data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Performance of a Contract: to create your account, authorize you, provide gameplay, and synchronize your progress.
  • Legitimate Interests: to fix technical bugs, protect the Game from fraud and cheating, and optimize performance.
  • Your Consent: to send you informational email newsletters.

5. Email Communications from the Administration

By providing your email address upon registration, you agree to receive electronic communications from the Game administration:

  • Transactional Messages: registration confirmation, password reset links, and critical security notifications. You cannot opt out of these emails, as they are necessary for account functionality.
  • Marketing Messages: news about Game updates, promotions, and in-game events. You can opt out of receiving these emails at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any such email.

6. Third-Party Services

We may use Software Development Kits (SDKs) from third-party partners for analytics, or infrastructure support. These companies may access your technical device identifiers or email address (only if the service is used for newsletters/authentication). Our Game uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase. To learn more about how Google processes your data, please visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

7. International Data Transfer

Your data may be transferred to and stored on servers located outside of your country or the European Economic Area (EEA). We ensure the protection of your data during such transfers by using Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission and applying robust encryption methods.

8. User Rights under GDPR and CCPA/CPRA

If you are a resident of the European Union (EU), the European Economic Area (EEA), or the State of California (USA), you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You can request a copy of the data collected about you (email, username).
  • Right to Rectification: You can request the correction of inaccurate data about yourself.
  • Right to Delete / "Right to be Forgotten": You can request the complete deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw your consent to marketing communications or data processing at any time.
  • CCPA Notice (Sale of Data): We do not sell your personal data (email, username) to third parties.

To exercise any of your rights, please contact us via email at: avdoss.support@gmail.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

9. Data retention

Data is stored as long as your Game account exists. You can permanently delete your account and all associated (login, email, game progress) at any time via the "Settings" menu inside the Game or by sending a request to our contact email. Data is deleted irretrievably.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage users to regularly check this page to stay informed about how we protect their information. Your continued use of the Game after changes are published constitutes your acceptance of the updated Policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via email at: avdoss.support@gmail.com
